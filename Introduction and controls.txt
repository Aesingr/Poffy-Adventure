  INTRODUCTION

Open the game in a browser, even offline, and press any key to start. The game uses its own dedicated voice: in that case put NVDA in focus mode and silence it (Insert S), or close it altogether. Any browser works, but I recommend Microsoft Edge, where the voice is faster and more responsive. Alternatively, with shift 9 you can play with your own screen reader (NVDA): in that case the NVDA option "report dynamic content changes" must be on (NVDA+5).

  GAME CONTROLS

up arrow, forward.
down arrow, backward.
left and right arrows, turn on the spot. If you are locked onto an enemy, you orbit around it.
shift held down, run.
shift plus left or right arrow, instant 90 degree turn.
space, jump on the spot.
space plus up arrow, jump forward.
shift in the air, flap your wings.
shift plus up arrow in the air, glide far. Release shift to stop gliding.
*for jumps and glides, it's best to hold space down until you land and then release it right away.

1, slower voice.
2, faster voice.
3, voice volume down.
4, voice volume up.
5, game volume down.
6, game volume up.
7, music volume down.
8, music volume up.
9, name and description of the level.
shift 9, switches between the game voice and your own screen reader (for example NVDA). it also works in the arcade, and the game remembers it. with the screen reader keep NVDA in focus mode; keys 1 to 4 no longer change the voice, which you set in NVDA.
0, opens the backpack. shift 0, describes the nearest character.

a, tells you the current element.
a plus left, right, up and down arrows scrolls through the elements.
b, tells you where you are (the surface or platform under your little paws).
c, talk to characters.
f, interact (universal).
g, turns you toward the last scanned target (from other keys such as h, j, y, u, i, o), telling you how far it is.
r, repeats the last scanned target, updated with distance and position.
*r is the manual version of g. It doesn't turn you toward the target, but gives extra information.
h, previous objective. shift h, previous object to observe.
j, next objective. shift j, next object to observe.
i, previous friend.
o, next friend.
k, your health points.
n, previous platform in the scanner.
m, next platform in the scanner.
v, distance scan: repeats the last scanned platform if it is within jumping or gliding reach, updated with distance and position.
p, use the active consumable. shift p, available crystals.
y, previous enemy.
u, next enemy.
q, nearest enemy. shift q, lock onto the nearest enemy.
*after locking on, the arrows change function and orbit around the target. Adding shift orbits quickly, which you need to face the bosses.
shift v, release the locked enemy.
e, basic attack. shift e, flying area attack.
t, parry.
*it can only be held down for a short time until you upgrade it at the forge.
w, shoot projectiles. shift w, charge a powerful projectile.
s, look ahead (scan).
x, look right (scan).
z, look left (scan).
*When the s scan says "jump" or "glide", it refers to the center of the scanned platform, not to the jump needed to reach it, unlike v, which refers to the edge of the scanned platform.
shift l, repeats the echo of the last dialogue you heard.
comma, previous checkpoint (where present in path levels).
period, next checkpoint (where present in path levels), or direction and distance from the center.
shift period, place and pick up a piece of furniture.
shift d, deliver a cooked dish.
*in swimming and flying levels, comma announces the distance from the ground or the surface. c turns upward, v downward, b levels you out, d lands or resurfaces if you are at most 3 meters from the ground or surface.
f7, save the game.
f9, load the game.

  VARIOUS TIPS

-Start calmly. And be patient. You'll get the hang of it.
-Give the game time to load all the sounds at startup before loading a game. Otherwise the loading music overlaps with the current one and all hell breaks loose.
-Start with the sapphire scale. Outside the little house, climb onto the banana bush, from there onto the orange tree, and from there onto the pineapple. This pattern works for every level: you start from the lowest platforms and climb onto the nearby higher ones. Some musical platforms, like the stars on Starry Beach, can't be found with g. Giving energy to the plants on Little Beach is useful for training with the sounds without using g, so only with r and v.
-To jump and glide, it's important to hold space down until you are on the new platform and then release it right away. Rely on the v scans to know whether you can reach the target.
-Save very often at strategic points, after a difficult jump or when you are close to a goal, like a scale or one of those nasty platforms. Good luck on the Snowy Mountain.
-Train your jumps and combat in the hole under the stone.
-Move a few steps after arriving in a new area before using the scan keys like h, j, i, o and so on. Otherwise you may pick up the interactables of the previous area.
-Adjust the volumes as you like. Sometimes the default isn't ideal for every audio device, which may boost the music or drown out the speech.
-Listen to the guide crystals, but not too much. They never waste themselves on explanations.
-Make good little dragon choices. I warned you.
-Find ways to cheat. I did.
-Keep a calendar handy. I have one.
-Keep in mind that there may be more than one way to approach the game. I'm not saying that at some point you might feel like starting all over again, but... I told you so.

  POFFY ARCADE

In the arcade you can face each other in several minigames from the game. With f1 you can read the controls of each game. At startup just press any key: the main menu opens, where left and right arrows choose between offline and online and enter confirms. Offline you choose the game with the arrows and play against the CPU. Online you can create a match or join one: whoever creates the match hears the code letter by letter, C copies it and R repeats it, and sends it to the other players; whoever joins types the code directly in the game (the arrows read back the characters, backspace deletes) and presses enter. Escape always takes you back, up to the main menu. In dungeons and arena, the skate park, windball, waiter and spudmole challenge, up to 4 players can play in total (one host and three guests). In booble fruit and salt ring, at most 2 can play.

Have a good game!
